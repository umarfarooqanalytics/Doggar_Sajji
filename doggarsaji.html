About Doggar Sajji

Where Tradition Meets Taste

Doggar Sajji is a family-run restaurant that brings the authentic taste of Balochistan to your table. We use traditional methods, secret spices, and fresh halal meat to create a Sajji experience like no other.

Our Sajji is slow-roasted over charcoal for hours, locking in the smoky flavor and tenderness that keeps our customers coming back for more.
